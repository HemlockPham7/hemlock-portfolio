## Brand & Style

The design system projects an expressive, forward-leaning creative portfolio identity. Built for modern digital creators, technologists, and design engineers, it establishes an atmosphere of digital precision paired with vibrant creative energy. The emotional response is intentional, confident, and technologically sophisticated.

The aesthetic merges modern minimalism with high-impact color transitions and soft glassmorphic depth. Crisp structural composition keeps the portfolio content central, while intense ultraviolet and electric indigo accents convey creative courage and technical mastery. High contrast between luminous violet-tinted surfaces and deep black ensures optimal legibility and focused impact.

## Colors

The palette balances intense digital chromas with pure black and airy tinted canvases:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Indigo Core):** Used for focal calls to action, active navigation indicators, key metrics, and progressive state accents.
- **Secondary (`#6B21A8` - Deep Violet):** Drives rich display headers, gradient pairings, and expressive badges, anchoring the design with editorial luxury.
- **Tertiary (`#A8E5FD` - Electric Cyan):** Acts as a high-visibility luminous counterweight for badges, interaction glow boundaries, and highlights on dark surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#000000` - Pure Black):** Provides dominant editorial text contrast, sharp button states, and deep grounding structures.
- **Surface Tint (`#F4F4FE`):** Delivers a subtle cool-indigo canvas that softens stark pure-white transitions and supports layered glass elevation.

Use color sparingly to direct gaze toward case studies, projects, and actionable contact points.

## Typography

Typography relies on a systematic, single-family architecture using Inter across all tiers. Visual variety is driven by high scale contrast, negative letter tracking on display elements, and strict weight variation.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked with negative letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, impactful editorial silhouettes typical of contemporary digital studios.
- **Body:** Neutral tracking and comfortable line heights ensure long-form project case studies remain fatigue-free.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or medium-weighted tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.06em`) delivers crisp mechanical readouts for tags, client categorizations, and metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum content container of 1280px.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Case study media and hero intros utilize multi-column spans (e.g., 8-column hero, 4-column side stats).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Complex dual-column case showcases collapse into alternating full-width cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem outer margins, 1rem gutters. All showcase grids stack sequentially to guarantee continuous vertical flow.

Spatial cadence follows an 8pt baseline rhythm. Component layouts favor generous white space around featured media to emphasize craftsmanship and project distinction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of low-contrast ghost outlines and soft, indigo-tinted ambient diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background rendered in `#F4F4FE`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface combined with a 1px border of `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)` and an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Elevates via a translateY(-4px) offset, expanding shadow to `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`, and sharpening the boundary outline.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Navigation):** Frosted glassmorphic layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur) bounded by `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` and drop shadow `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a Level 2 (Rounded) curvature grammar. Standard interactive elements (input fields, action buttons, filter tags) adopt an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.

Portfolio cards and media containers scale up to `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) and `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`), softening the geometry of high-density screenshots and graphic artifacts. Pill variants are reserved strictly for standalone meta-tags, pill buttons, and status indicator chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#000000` with `#FFFFFF` text for maximum authority, shifting to `#4F46E5` on hover with a subtle scale transform (`1.02`). Radius set to 8px; horizontal padding 20px, height 44px.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in `#000000`. Inverts to black background with white text on hover.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure text accompanied by an animated directional arrow icon, accenting in `#4F46E5` on focus or hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Metadata tags leverage pill silhouettes (`9999px` radius).
- Background set to `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)` with `#4F46E5` text. Selected or active filter states switch to filled `#6B21A8` with white text.

### Cards (Project Showcase)
- Composed of an elevated Level 1 white surface with an internal padding of `space-lg` (24px).
- Incorporates an image viewport with an aspect ratio of 16:10, clipped by a 12px internal radius.
- Includes a bottom title lockup with category labels in `label-sm` and project title in `headline-sm`. Hover transitions zoom the media slightly (`1.04` scale over 350ms ease-out).

### Input Fields
- Enclosed with a 1px border in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)` on a pure white surface.
- Focus states apply a 2px active ring in `#4F46E5` with a soft cyan ambient glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(168, 229, 253, 0.4)`).
- Labels are anchored above using `label-md` in neutral black.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px form with 4px border radius for checkboxes; circular 18px form for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`.
- Checked: Solid `#4F46E5` fill with a white checkmark or inner dot, triggering a gentle scale pop animation.

### Lists & Timeline Trackers
- Minimalist timeline or experience lists framed by a left-aligned 2px continuous line in `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- Active nodes marked by an 8px solid `#4F46E5` disc ringed by a 4px halo in `#A8E5FD`.
